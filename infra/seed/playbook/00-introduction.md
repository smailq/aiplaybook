# Your Marketing Playbook

Welcome to your living playbook for **Awareness3**.

This book is co-authored in the open by three people:

- **The author** - an AI marketing strategist that researches, plans, and drafts.
- **The guide** - a human expert who edits the book and gives direction to both you and the AI.
- **You** - the customer, who runs the experiments the book proposes and reports back what happened.

The book is never finished. Each page is versioned on its own, like a software release, and improves as evidence comes in. Read it front to back once, then treat it as the plan you execute against.

## How to use it

1. Read the current pages.
2. Use the **Ask** box to talk to the author - request a plan, a draft, or a critique.
3. Run what you agree on, then report the results so the next version of the page reflects reality.

The north-star metric for this playbook is **qualified waitlist signups** for the Awareness3 macOS private beta, and their conversion into activated beta users.
