Welcome to your living playbook for **Awareness3**.

This book is co-authored in the open by three people:

- **The author** - an AI marketing strategist that researches, plans, and drafts.
- **The guide** - a human expert who edits the book and gives direction to both you and the AI.
- **You** - the customer, who runs the experiments the book proposes and reports back what happened.

The book is never finished.
Each section is versioned on its own, like a software release, and improves as evidence comes in.
Read it front to back once, then treat it as the plan you execute against.
