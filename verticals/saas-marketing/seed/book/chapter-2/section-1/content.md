**Product:** Awareness3, a local-first macOS app that reads the documents, bills, receipts, and emails you drop in, files them for you, and reminds you about what actually needs doing.

**Tagline in use:** "Stop filing. Start knowing."

**Hero line:** "Most apps just store your stuff. This one actually understands it."

## Who it is for

The first hypothesis: individuals and solo operators drowning in personal and small-business paperwork who want the filing and the follow-ups to just happen, on a Mac, without their data leaving the device.

## Why it is different

- **Local-first** - the reading and understanding happen on your machine, not in someone's cloud.
- **Understands, not just stores** - it extracts the dates, amounts, names, and companies that matter and turns them into records plus reminders.
- **Markdown substrate** - the knowledge it builds is open and portable, readable by both humans and tools.
